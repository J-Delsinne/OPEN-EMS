First-time access — accepting the security certificate

OPEN-EMS runs in your home and uses an in-home security certificate. The first time you open the dashboard from a new device or browser, you will see a warning that says "Not Secure" or "Your connection is not private". This is expected — the warning means your browser is checking that you trust the system on your local network. Accept it once on each device you use, and the warning will not reappear unless the certificate is re-issued.

One-time acceptance per browser

  • Chrome / Edge: click "Advanced", then "Proceed to <site>".
  • Safari: click "Show Details", then "visit this website", and confirm.
  • Firefox: click "Advanced", then "Accept the Risk and Continue".

Once accepted, the warning will not reappear unless the certificate is re-issued.

The dashboard at a glance

When you open the dashboard, you will see a status sentence at the top and a small group of cards below it. Here is what each part shows:

Status headline
A single sentence describing what the system is doing right now — for example, "Your home is running on solar · Minimize Cost".
Battery card
How much of your battery is currently charged, in percent.
Solar card
How much electricity your panels are producing right now, in kilowatts (kW).
Grid card
How much electricity is flowing to or from the public grid, in kilowatts. A positive number means you are drawing from the grid; a negative number means you are exporting.
EV card
The next scheduled EV charging session, and a "Charge EV now" button to start charging immediately.
{# Section 5 — How to change your energy strategy. Three-step walkthrough for Story 10.3. #}

How to change your energy strategy

  1. Tap the strategy text in the status headline.
  2. Pick one of the three options:
    • Minimize Cost — the system tries to use the cheapest electricity available across the day.
    • Maximize Self-Consumption — the system prefers your own solar and battery before drawing from the grid.
    • Prioritize EV — the system gives the car charger priority when there is energy available.
  3. The status headline updates within a few seconds to confirm the new strategy is active.
{# Section 6 — Degraded-mode messages and when to call the installer. #}

Understanding "limited functionality" messages and when to call your installer

The dashboard sometimes shows "Running with limited functionality" instead of the normal status sentence. This is normal — the system is still safe, and you can continue using the dashboard. It usually means one device is briefly unavailable and the rest of the system is carrying on without it.

Call your installer if:

  • The "limited functionality" message stays visible for more than 24 hours.
  • One of the cards shows "Unavailable" for a specific device.
  • You cannot reach the dashboard at all from a browser that has worked before.

You do not need to call about brief, automatic recovery — the system handles short device hiccups on its own.
